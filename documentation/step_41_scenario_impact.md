# Step 41 — Scenario impact analysis

The SRS asks the application to show simulated impact on revenue, contribution margin, demand, wastage and profitability, clearly distinguishing estimates from actual results. Step 41 extends the read-only Step 40 page at `/locations/scenarios/`. It derives a standardized indicator table from the exact Step 40 simulation result; it does not fit a new model or change source records.

Every indicator shows its baseline, simulated estimate, change, unit, status and interpretation. Status is `Estimated`, `Held constant`, `Estimated proxy` or `Not estimated`. The comparison basis is stated: accepted 90-day historical data ending 2026-06-30, except the predicted-demand scenario, which uses a saved future item/location forecast and is not an observed actual. The page retains the prominent **Simulated estimate · not actual** label and Step 40's assumptions/caveat.

For item price and discount scenarios, the user's assumed demand response drives portions, revenue and direct contribution; recorded waste stays constant because no waste-response model exists. Removal is an extreme no-substitution assumption: item sales and recorded waste go to zero, but simulated direct margin is undefined at zero revenue and shown as `Not estimated`. Preparation and wastage scenarios hold accepted sales, revenue and direct contribution constant while changing recorded waste. They separately show `Contribution after recorded waste (proxy) = direct contribution − accepted recorded waste cost`; this is **not net accounting profit** and does not reconcile missing inventory or fixed overhead. Direct margin is contribution/revenue, also not net profit.

Promotion frequency uses observed tagged-order averages and assumes extra orders are incremental. Its waste effect is `Not estimated`, since no causal campaign-to-waste model exists. The predicted-demand scenario shows future portions only; future revenue, contribution, wastage and margin are explicitly `Not estimated` because future item price, cost and mix were not forecast. No estimated amount is presented as guaranteed savings or uplift.

Verification: `uv run python dine_iq/manage.py test apps.restaurants.test_scenarios apps.restaurants.test_recommendations` checks all eight scenario impact tables, forecast abstention, waste-cost proxy separation, zero-revenue margin, location access and page labels. `uv run python dine_iq/manage.py check` validates Django configuration.
