## Human sign-off

The reviewer should record their name, date, reviewed files, corrections made,
commands run, findings and unresolved issues. Then update the corresponding
`AI_USAGE.md` entry. Automated tests alone do not satisfy independent human
understanding. Preserve unresolved findings rather than marking every item pass.

Rehearse one evaluator-selected change, such as a new location, threshold,
feature, KPI or filter, and record the actual explanation and verification.
