# Step 40 — What-if scenario analysis

The SRS asks users to simulate price increases/decreases, discount changes, promotion frequency, item removal, preparation reduction, predicted-demand increases and wastage assumptions. A login-required, read-only Django page at `/locations/scenarios/` supports all eight. It uses the verified Step 9 90-day item/location snapshot, Step 33 location promotion totals and Step 20 July–September item/location forecasts. The cutoff is 2026-07-01. Users can only choose restaurants granted through the existing location-access policy. The page performs calculations in memory; it does not write orders, prices, inventory, promotions or predictions.

Inputs are bounded. Price changes are 0.1–30%; the user separately supplies an assumed demand response from −50% to +50%. Discounts can change by −30 to +30 percentage points, but the resulting assumed discount must remain 0–60%; the same user-entered demand response applies. The latest list price determines the per-portion adjustment, while accepted 90-day net revenue and direct cost per portion are the baseline. This is not an estimated price elasticity.

Promotion frequency can increase 0.1–100% of observed tagged orders at the selected location. The scenario assumes added orders are incremental and carry the same observed per-tagged-order revenue and direct contribution; it does not account for cannibalization or new campaign spend. Removing an item sets its historical item-specific sales, contribution and waste to zero under an extreme no-substitution assumption; it is not a removal recommendation. Preparation reduction is 0.1–50% of recorded prepared portions and is rejected when the reduction exceeds recorded waste, since unchanged sales would then be unsupported. Wastage assumption changes are −50% to +100% of accepted recorded waste, holding accepted sales constant. Predicted demand can increase 0.1–100% relative to a saved 1–3 month Step 20 forecast; this scenario displays portions only rather than inventing future prices or revenue.

The result card labels every number **Simulated estimate · not actual**, shows baseline, estimate and change, and lists the assumptions and a specific caveat. Direct contribution excludes overhead, platform fees and, in these scenarios, inventory/waste accounting unless the metric is separately shown. Accepted-data gaps, synthetic seasonality and Step 20 forecast error remain. Step 41 will formalize broader scenario-impact analysis; Step 40 is the interactive bounded calculator.

Verification: `uv run python dine_iq/manage.py test apps.restaurants.test_scenarios` covers all eight formulas, invalid bounds and location authorization. `uv run python dine_iq/manage.py check` must also pass.
