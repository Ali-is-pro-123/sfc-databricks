# Step 42 — Executive dashboard

The SRS asks for total revenue, total profit, orders, AOV, active and repeat customers, wastage, forecast demand, critical recommendations and anomalies. The login-required `/dashboard/` page presents those measures for the user's permitted restaurant locations, with a location comparison and ordering-channel table for context. It uses the existing Tailwind CDN base template and performs no analytical writes.

Historical cards cover restaurant-local dates from 2026-04-02 inclusive to 2026-07-01 exclusive. Revenue, direct cost/contribution, completed order headers and accepted waste come from the reconciled Step 33 location snapshot. AOV is **scoped total revenue ÷ scoped completed orders**, not an average of location AOVs; completed empty accepted baskets remain in the denominator. The SRS says “total profit,” but overhead and delivery-platform fees are unavailable, so the dashboard clearly displays **direct contribution as a profit proxy**, not net accounting profit. Wastage is accepted recorded portions and cost; inventory reconciliation gaps remain.

Active customers are distinct identified customers with at least one Step 7 customer-metric-eligible completed order in the window across all accessible locations. Repeat customers have at least two eligible orders across those locations. They are counted after scoping, so a customer visiting two accessible locations is not double-counted. This executive definition differs from Step 33's per-location repeat rate, which requires two orders at the same restaurant.

Forecast demand is the sum of Step 20 July 2026 item/location portions for accessible restaurants. It is a future estimate, not an actual. Critical recommendations count accessible location-specific Step 39 review candidates, plus global candidates only for analysts/administrators; no current candidate meets the narrow Critical gate. Anomalies count Step 31 flagged **location-days** in the historical 90-day window, not unique locations or proven errors. Channel and location rows reconcile to the scoped revenue/order totals.

The dashboard checks passed source reports, matching 2026-07-01 cutoffs, source row counts, successful order-feature output, scoped order and channel reconciliation, and full-scope Step 33 totals. It returns an explicit unavailable state when verified sources are missing or inconsistent. Django's existing `visible_restaurants` access boundary governs the page: managers see only granted locations, analysts/administrators see the locations available to their role, and unauthenticated requests redirect to login.

Verification: `uv run python dine_iq/manage.py test apps.restaurants.test_dashboard` covers full-data reconciliation, subset aggregation, customer de-duplication bounds and role scoping. `uv run python dine_iq/manage.py check` validates the application. On the full synthetic snapshot, the page computes PKR 125,910,828.14 revenue, 73,982 completed orders, 36,249 active customers, 20,721 repeat customers, about 133,225 July forecast portions, zero Critical recommendations and 136 flagged location-days. These are analytical snapshot values, not live operational figures.
